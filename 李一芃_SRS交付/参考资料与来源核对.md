# 参考资料汇总与来源核对

第24组｜李一芃汇总｜2026年10月20日

本次整理两位组员提供的3项候选资料，并补充2篇与AI人机交接和工具调用相关的论文，共2本领域书籍、3篇文章。每项英文注释单独满足30～60 words口径，中文说明补充使用位置与核对范围。文献支持方法选择，不构成 StudyFlow 的实测效果证据。

## R01 领域书籍：用例写作

Cockburn, A. (2001). *Writing Effective Use Cases*. Addison-Wesley Professional. ISBN 978-0-201-70225-5.

来源：[出版社书目与介绍](https://www.informit.com/store/writing-effective-use-cases-9780201702255)。

**English annotation**

> This book explains how actors, goals, preconditions, success scenarios, and extensions structure effective use cases. StudyFlow uses this framework to describe scheduling and plan confirmation, particularly the exceptions involving stale proposals, changed permissions, and failed operations. It supports specification structure rather than product performance claims.

提供者：雍致鸣。核对出版社作者、ISBN、目录及公开介绍；页面列出出版时间2000年、版权2001年，参考条目沿用版权年2001。未声称通读全书。使用位置：雍致鸣正文第3/4节，本人正文第2/3节。

## R02 领域书籍：Web可用性

Krug, S. (2014). *Don't Make Me Think, Revisited: A Common Sense Approach to Web Usability* (3rd ed.). New Riders. ISBN 978-0-321-96551-6.

来源：[出版社书目与介绍](https://www.informit.com/store/dont-make-me-think-revisited-a-common-sense-approach-9780321965516)。

**English annotation**

> Krug presents practical principles for understandable web navigation, concise information, and usability testing. StudyFlow applies these ideas when exposing requirement sources, pending fields, and delivery gaps. The book also motivates observing students completing realistic tasks instead of treating attractive mockups as evidence that the workflow is usable.

提供者：董泽福。核对作者、第3版、ISBN及公开目录；页面出版日期2013年12月24日、版权2014年，采用版权年2014。未声称通读全书。使用位置：董泽福正文第5节原型说明；本包用户任务测试设计的方法背景。

## R03 文章：战略分析

Weihrich, H. (1982). The TOWS matrix—A tool for situational analysis. *Long Range Planning, 15*(2), 54–66. DOI: [10.1016/0024-6301(82)90120-0](https://doi.org/10.1016/0024-6301(82)90120-0)。

核对来源：[大学教学站点所存论文原文](https://elearning15.unibg.it/pluginfile.php/644921/mod_folder/content/0/STR%20T06%20Weihrich_1982_LRP.pdf)。

**English annotation**

> Weihrich connects external opportunities and threats with internal strengths and weaknesses to formulate strategic actions. StudyFlow uses this method to relate its small team, uncertain AI performance, and educational focus to scope choices, manual fallback, and staged delivery. The framework organizes decisions; it does not validate market demand.

提供者：董泽福。核对论文首页作者、题名、卷期页码及正文对内外因素配对的说明。出版社页面本次读取受限，使用原论文副本交叉核对，保留原稿DOI；不把无法访问的出版社页面写成已通读。使用位置：董泽福正文第3节TOWS行动矩阵。

## R04 文章：人机交接与纠错

Amershi, S., Weld, D., Vorvoreanu, M., Fourney, A., Nushi, B., Collisson, P., Suh, J., Iqbal, S., Bennett, P. N., Inkpen, K., Teevan, J., Kikin-Gil, R., & Horvitz, E. (2019). Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction. *Proceedings of the 2019 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems*. ACM. DOI: [10.1145/3290605.3300233](https://doi.org/10.1145/3290605.3300233)。

来源：[Microsoft Research论文页](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/)，[作者公开稿](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/Guidelines-for-Human-AI-Interaction-camera-ready.pdf)。

**English annotation**

> This paper proposes interaction guidelines for communicating AI capabilities, handling errors, and supporting correction. StudyFlow uses them to motivate visible uncertainty, editable suggestions, and understandable recovery paths. Its scheduling proposals remain subject to explicit human confirmation and deterministic validation; these safeguards are project requirements, not outcomes established by the paper.

提供者：李一芃。本次核对作者机构论文页、摘要及公开稿，采用其人机交互方法作设计参考，不将论文其他产品的研究结果当成本项目指标。使用位置：本人正文第1/4/5节，UC-06异常、UI-03错误恢复及量化验证E3。

## R05 文章：模型与工具交互

Yao, S., Zhao, J., Yu, D., Du, N., Shafran, I., Narasimhan, K., & Cao, Y. (2023). ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models. *The Eleventh International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR 2023)*. [arXiv:2210.03629](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629)。

**English annotation**

> ReAct studies language models that combine reasoning with actions and observations from external environments. It informs StudyFlow's bounded sequence of authorized queries, scheduling simulation, and proposal creation. The project separately enforces tool permissions and plan constraints, because a model's fluent explanation cannot establish schedule feasibility or authorize a formal update.

提供者：李一芃。核对作者、题名、摘要及作者存档的ICLR camera-ready版本说明；初始预印本发表于2022年，本条采用会议年2023。OpenReview本次触发浏览器验证，未声称已读取评审；作者存档用于核验。使用位置：本人正文第1/4/5节工具流程。项目不要求公开或持久保存模型内部推理过程，只保存结构化输入依据、调用摘要与结果。
